# Dogear logo brief

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **From:** /spec dogear-brand, pause 2

## The product

Dogear is a Forge app for Jira. It keeps a short, current note on each ticket, a dogear, so
that a teammate or their coding agent carries on from where the work stopped.

The name: a dog-ear is the folded corner of a page. It marks where a reader stopped.

Tagline: "Mark where you stopped."

## Who sees the mark

- Codegeist judges, who are Atlassian people, on the install screen and in the video.
- Software teams, in the header of a panel on a Jira ticket, at about 16 pixels.

## Character

Quiet, exact, dependable, and a little warm. The name is friendly, so the mark can smile.
The panel is a work tool, so the mark must not shout.

## Where it must work

- 16 pixels in a Jira panel header, in the light theme and in the dark theme.
- The app's tile on Atlassian's install and consent screens.
- The Devpost thumbnail, the README header and the title card of the video.
- One colour, and reversed.

## What to stay away from

- The plain file icon: a page with a folded corner and lines of text. It is the first
  picture that the name gives, and each operating system has one.
- Atlassian's own marks: the blue chevrons of Jira, and Atlassian blue as the main colour.
- The usual icons of Jira apps: a white glyph on a blue tile, a tick, a list, a gear, a
  sticky note, and sparkles for AI.
- Dog marks that exist: a dog profile cut out of a square (Hound), a dog's face (Snyk, Pug).

## Concepts

Twelve ideas in one line each. The three that were drawn are in bold.

1. **Fold-ear: a dog in profile, and its ear is the folded corner of a page.**
2. **Folded D: the letter D, with its top-left corner dog-eared.**
3. **Corner: only the corner of a page, as two edges and the fold.**
4. A Jira ticket card with a folded corner and one line of text.
5. The word "dogear", with the corner of the "d" folded.
6. A fold that shows a second colour under it: the next person.
7. A folded corner that reads as an arrow: carry on from here.
8. One floppy ear alone, as a rounded triangle.
9. A bookmark ribbon with a folded tip.
10. A page with two corners: one up, one folded, as two ears.
11. A solid D that is also a page, with a folded corner.
12. A fold whose crease makes a tick.

Idea 4 is the plain file icon. Ideas 6 and 9 need colour to read. Ideas 8 and 12 need a
caption. Idea 10 loses its ears at 16 pixels.

## Round 2

Dzafran asked for a new round on 2026-11-23. His answers: show a dog and a mark with no dog,
lean warm and friendly when the character words pull apart, and the colour is open.

| Concept | File | The idea |
| ------- | ---- | -------- |
| A, Page pup | `round-2/a-page-pup.svg` | A page with a face. Its folded corner is the ear. |
| B, Origami pup | `round-2/b-origami-pup.svg` | A dog's face folded from one sheet. Both ears are folds. |
| C, Folded d | `round-2/c-folded-d.svg` | A page with one corner folded. It also reads as the letter d. |

`round-2/concepts.png` shows the three on one sheet, each with a lockup and at 64, 32 and 16
pixels. The letters of "dogear" in the lockups are drawn shapes, not a font.

Tested at 16, 24 and 32 pixels, in black on white and in white on Jira's dark grey. Page pup
and Folded d hold at 16 pixels. Origami pup is clear from 24 pixels, and its ears blur at 16.

Chosen on 2026-11-23: A, Page pup. The kit is in `kit/`, and `kit/guidelines.md` says how to
use it. The colour is Ginger `#E55F12`, with Ink `#231A14` and Paper `#FFF6EA`.

Dropped in this round: a "d" with a floppy top (it reads as a music note), a circle with a
folded edge (it reads as a sticker), and a face with one ear up and one ear folded.
